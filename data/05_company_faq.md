# Acme Store Help Center

## Returns
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in the original packaging. Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $4.99. Orders over $50 ship free. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99. We ship to the United States and Canada only. Tracking numbers are emailed once the order leaves the warehouse.

## Order changes and cancellations
You can cancel or change an order within 1 hour of placing it from the Orders page. After that the order enters packing and cannot be changed. If it is too late, wait for delivery and start a return.

## Payments
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. Gift cards can be combined with one other payment method. Payment is charged when the order ships, not when it is placed. If you see two charges, one is usually a temporary authorization that disappears in 3 days.

## Warranty
Electronics include a 12 month warranty against manufacturing defects. Damage from drops, water, or misuse is not covered. To claim, contact support with your order number and a photo of the problem. Furniture has a 24 month warranty on structural parts.

## Account and security
Reset your password from the Sign In page using the Forgot Password link. We never ask for your password by email or phone. Turn on two factor authentication in Account Settings for extra safety.

## Contact support
Support is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Eastern Time. Email support@acme.example or use live chat on the website. Average first response time is under 4 hours on business days. For urgent delivery problems, choose the Urgent option in the contact form.

## Loyalty program
Silver members earn 1 point per dollar, Gold members earn 2 points per dollar. 100 points equal $1 in store credit. Points expire after 12 months of no activity. Members reach Gold after spending $1,000 in a year.
